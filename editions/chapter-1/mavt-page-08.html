Sthiramati, Madhyāntavibhāgaṭīkā — Yamaguchi p. 8

OCR lines 1392–1423. Methodology: mavt-reedition-workflow.md. Tibetan witness: BO_T06_D4032:192b-19 → 193a-30.

Page 8 completes the first explanation of the saptārtha order (begun on p. 7: bhāvanā → avasthā → phala → yāna-anuttarya), then presents two alternative explanations of the order: anyas tv āha ("another view says": saṁkleśa = āvaraṇa, vyavadāna = tattva; the order goes via āvaraṇa-tattva-bhāvanā-avasthā-phala-yānānuttarya); aparaḥ punar āhuḥ ("yet another says": lakṣaṇa first as sad-asad-lakṣaṇa-jñāpana, then āvaraṇa-tattva as prahāṇa/sākṣātkaraṇa, then bhāvanā as upāya, avasthā as tāratamya, phala as prahāṇa-result, yāna-anuttarya last to manifest the asādhāraṇa-mahāyāna-naya). The mid-page sūtra-citation ("that renunciant trains in śrāvaka-, pratyekabuddha-, and bodhisattva- śikṣā-conduct") sets up the contrast with what is non-shared for bodhisattvas alone.

§1–§2 — completing the first anukrama explanation: pratipakṣa-bhāvanā + avasthā VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (MS)

āvaraṇaṁ yena prayogeṇa kṣayo bhavati sā pratipakṣa-bhāvanā jñeyā | tatas tasyāṁ pratipakṣa-bhāvanāyāṁ vipakṣa-hānyā pratipakṣa-vṛddhyā cāvasthā jñeyā gotrāvasthādikā | (OCR 1394–1397)

No change

A clean MS-attested completion. The chain āvaraṇa-prahāṇa → pratipakṣa-bhāvanā → avasthā (graded by vipakṣa-hāni + pratipakṣa-vṛddhi) → gotrāvasthā-ādikā is the canonical Yogācāra path-stage formulation.

Translation

The praxis by which the obscuration is destroyed is to be known as pratipakṣa-bhāvanā. Then, in that pratipakṣa-bhāvanā, the avasthā (gradation) is to be known by the decline of the contrary side and the growth of the [right] side — beginning with the gotra-stage.

§3 — phalāni: orientation toward the lokottara-dharma VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (italic)

tato lokottara-dharma-ābhimukhīkaraṇaṁ phalāni | srotaāpanna-phalādīni veditavyāni | sarvam apy etat sottarāc chrāvakādibhiḥ sādhāraṇam bodhisattvānāṁ [continues §4] (OCR 1397–1399)

No change

A standard formulation: phala = lokottara-dharma-ābhimukhīkaraṇam ("the [stage of] orientation toward the lokottara dharma"). The qualification sottarāt ("up through the [anāgāmin]") on the śrāvaka-side reflects the standard four-fruit (srotāpatti → sakṛd-āgāmin → anāgāmin → arhattva) enumeration where bodhisattvas share with śrāvakas up to anāgāmin but the fourth fruit (arhattva) is non-shared. Reconstruction is philologically well-grounded.

Translation

Then [come] the fruits — the orientation toward the supramundane dharma; the fruit of stream-entry and so on are to be known. All this is common to bodhisattvas with the [up-through-anāgāmin] śrāvakas.

§4–§5 — sūtra-citation + asādhāraṇa: bodhisattvānāṁ tv ānuttaryam VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (MS + italic + MS)

yathoktaṁ sūtre — sa pravrajitaḥ śrāvaka-śikṣācāra-gocara-samudācāram api śikṣate pratyekabuddha-śikṣācāra-gocara-samudācāram api śikṣate bodhisattva-śikṣācāra-gocara-samudācāram api śikṣate | bodhisattvānāṁ tv ānuttaryam asādhāraṇam saptamo 'rtha ānuttaryam | (OCR 1399–1403)

No change

The sūtra-citation is verbatim Yogācāra boilerplate (cf. Bodhisattvabhūmi, ed. Wogihara, p. 76 ff.). The completion bodhisattvānāṁ tv ānuttaryam asādhāraṇam ("for bodhisattvas, however, the unsurpassed-ness is non-shared") is exactly Sthiramati's idiom and the only philological way to introduce the seventh topic. Tib (193a-17 head): thun mong ma yin pa ste don bdun pa bla na med pa'o.

Translation

As said in the sūtra: "That renunciant trains in the conduct, the field, and the practice of the śrāvaka's training; also in those of the pratyekabuddha's training; also in those of the bodhisattva's training." For bodhisattvas, however, the unsurpassed-ness is non-shared — [hence] the seventh topic is "unsurpassed-ness".

§6–§8 — alternative view 1 (anyas tv āha): saṁkleśa=āvaraṇa, vyavadāna=tattva VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (italic + MS)

anyas tv āha | saṁkleśa-vyavadāna-lakṣaṇe kauśal-otpādan-ārtham ādau lakṣaṇam | tatra yaḥ saṁkleśas tad āvaraṇam | yad vyavadānaṁ tat tattvam | āvaraṇa-prahāṇaṁ ca tattvādhigamād bhavatīty ata āvaraṇa-tattvam | (OCR 1404–1407)

Tibetan

གཞན་ན་རེ་ཀུན་ནས་ཉོན་མོངས་པ་དང་། རྣམ་པར་བྱང་བའི་མཚན་ཉིད་ལ་མཁས་པར་བྱ་བ་བསྐྱེད་པའི་ཕྱིར་ཐོག་མར་མཚན་ཉིད་དོ། ། དེ་ལ་ཀུན་ནས་ཉོན་མོངས་པ་གང་ཡིན་པ་དེ་ནི་སྒྲིབ་པའོ། ། རྣམ་པར་བྱང་བ་གང་ཡིན་པ་དེ་ནི་དེ་ཁོ་ནའོ། ། དེ་ཁོ་ན་ཉིད་ཁོང་དུ་ཆུད་པས་སྒྲིབ་པ་སྤོང་བར་འགྱུར་ཏེ། དེའི་ཕྱིར་སྒྲིབ་པ་དང་དེ་ཁོ་ནའོ། ། (BO_T06_D4032:193a-17)

No change

A clean alternative view that maps the saptārtha sequence onto the saṁkleśa/vyavadāna dichotomy. Tibetan matches the reconstruction step-by-step: gzhan na re = anyas tv āha; kun nas nyon mongs pa dang rnam par byang ba'i mtshan nyid la mkhas par bya ba bskyed pa'i phyir thog mar mtshan nyid do = saṁkleśa-vyavadāna-lakṣaṇe kauśal-otpādan-ārtham ādau lakṣaṇam.

Translation

Yet another view says: in order to generate skill in the saṁkleśa-lakṣaṇa and vyavadāna-lakṣaṇa, lakṣaṇa [is taught] first. Of these, that which is saṁkleśa is the obscuration; that which is vyavadāna is the truth. And since the abandonment of obscuration comes from the realisation of truth, [the next pair is] āvaraṇa-tattva.

§9–§12 — alternative view 1 cont'd: mārga → avasthā → phala → yāna-anuttarya VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (italic + MS)

tat-prahāṇopāya-pradarśanārthaṁ tataḥ saparivāra-pratipakṣo mārgaḥ | tasya mārgasyādi-madhya-anteṣu mṛdu-madhya-adhimātra-prabhedānāṁ pradarśanārtham tatrāvasthā |(1) avasthānurūpaṁ phalam āvahatīti tadanantaraṁ phalam | etac ca sarvaṁ bodhisattvasya śrāvakādi-sādhāraṇam ity asādhāraṇa-mahāyāna-naya-prakāśanārthaṁ yānānuttaryam iti | (OCR 1407–1413)

n. 1: MS avasthāvānurūpaṁ; Tib gnas skabs dang mthun pa.

One MS slip to fix: avasthāvānurūpaṁ → avasthānurūpaṁ

The MS has the slip avasthāvānurūpaṁ (insertion of -vā-); Tib gnas skabs dang mthun pa ("conforming to the stage") = avasthā-anurūpa. Yamaguchi has silently corrected this. Accept.

Translation

In order to show the means for abandoning that [obscuration], then [comes] the path together with its retinue — the pratipakṣa; to display the divisions (mild/medium/keen) at the beginning, middle, and end of that path — there [comes] avasthā. Since [the avasthā] brings about the fruit corresponding to itself, immediately after [comes] phala. And all this is common to the bodhisattva with the śrāvakas and so on; therefore, to make manifest the non-shared way of the Great Vehicle, [comes the seventh:] yāna-anuttarya.

§13–§14 — alternative view 2 (aparaḥ punar āhuḥ): sad-asad-lakṣaṇa first VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (italic + MS)

aparaḥ punar āhuḥ | sad-asad-lakṣaṇa-jñāpanārthaṁ prathamato lakṣaṇam ākhyātam | lakṣaṇaṁ jñātvāvaraṇaṁ prahātavyaṁ tattvaṁ ca sākṣātkartavyam iti tadanantaraṁ āvaraṇa-tattvam | (OCR 1414–1416)

Tibetan

གཞན་དག་ན་རེ་ཡོད་པ་དང་མེད་པའི་མཚན་ཉིད་ཤེས་པར་བྱ་བའི་ཕྱིར་ཐོག་མར་མཚན་ཉིད་བཤད་དོ། ། མཚན་ཉིད་ཤེས་ནས་སྒྲིབ་པ་སྤང་བར་བྱ་བ་དང་། དེ་ཁོ་ན་མངོན་སུམ་དུ་བྱ་བས་དེའི་འོག་ཏུ་སྒྲིབ་པ་དང་དེ་ཁོ་ནའོ། ། (BO_T06_D4032:193a-30)

One micro-detail: singular vs plural āhuḥ

Tibetan gzhan dag na re uses the plural-particle dag, which Yamaguchi correctly renders with the plural āhuḥ ("they say"). Contrast with §6 above where gzhan na re (no dag) is rendered anyas tv āha (singular). The pattern reveals Sthiramati uses singular for the first alternative view and plural for the second. No change.

Translation

Yet others say: in order to make known the sat- and asat-lakṣaṇa, lakṣaṇa is expounded first. Having known the lakṣaṇa, the obscuration is to be abandoned and the truth is to be realised — so immediately after [comes] āvaraṇa-tattva.

§15–§17 — alternative view 2 cont'd: bhāvanā → avasthā → phala → yāna-anuttarya VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (MS + italic ending)

tayoś ca prahāṇa-sākṣātkaraṇayor ayam upāya iti pratipakṣa-bhāvanā | tasyāś ca tāratamyam avasthāviśeṣaḥ |(2) tat kṛtaṁ ca prahāṇaṁ phalam | tadanantaraṁ yānānuttaryam iti prakāśanārtham ayam anukrama iti | (OCR 1417–1420)

n. 2: Tib des spang bar bya ba = tat prahātavyam.

One textual variant to note

Tibetan n. 2 records des spang bar bya ba = tat prahātavyam ("that-to-be-abandoned"), where Yamaguchi's Sanskrit reads tat kṛtaṁ ca prahāṇaṁ ("and the accomplished abandonment"). Both make sense in the phala-context, but they say slightly different things: Yamaguchi's emphasises the completed elimination (phala = accomplished prahāṇa), while the Tibetan emphasises the thing to be abandoned. Sanskrit reading is more idiomatic for the phala-defining context; keep as printed and note variant.

Translation

For these two [activities — abandonment and realisation —] the means is this: pratipakṣa-bhāvanā. The gradation of that is avasthā-viśeṣa. And the accomplished elimination is the phala. Following that, yāna-anuttarya [comes], to manifest [the non-shared way of the Great Vehicle]. So this is the [reason for the] order.
