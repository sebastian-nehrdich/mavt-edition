Page 10 is the first page of chapter 1 (Lakṣaṇa-pariccheda). It opens with the bhāṣya's introductory phrase tatra lakṣaṇam ārabhyāha, then cites MAV kārikā I.1 in full, then begins Sthiramati's exegesis: (a) tatra = "among the seven things"; (b) the topic taken up first is lakṣaṇa; (c) refutation of the sarvāpavāda view (sc. that all dharmas are niḥsvabhāva like a hare's horn); (d) introduction of abhūta-parikalpaḥ as that which IS — qualified by svabhāvataḥ; (e) the nanv evaṃ sūtra-virodhaḥ objection (the sūtras say all dharmas are empty — how can this be compatible?); (f) reply via the kārikā's second pāda dvayam tatra na vidyate: the abhūta-parikalpa is "empty" in the specific sense of grāhya-grāhaka-svarūpa-rahitatā, not in the absolute niḥsvabhāva sense; (g) anticipation of the next-stage objection (śūnyatā-abhāva-prasaṅga) and pointer to the kārikā's third pāda śūnyatā vidyate tv atra as the answer.

For chapter 1 the existing file mavt-ch1-commentary.html has 20 detailed sentence sections through OCR line ~1900; sections §1–§8 of that file cover OCR lines 1451–1470 (= page 10 main text). The findings are summarised below.

§1 — bhāṣya-pratīka: tatra lakṣaṇam ārabhyāha VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text

tatra lakṣaṇam ārabhyāha (OCR 1451)

No change

Verbatim quotation of MAVBh SA_T06_vmvkbh_u:15. Tib MAVBh (BO_T06_D4027:1b-18) and Tib MAVT (BO_T06_D4032:193b-12) both render de la mtshan nyid las brtsams nas. The formula lakṣaṇam ārabhyāha is unique to MAVBh (no other Yogācāra commentary uses this exact introductory turn — confirmed by /primary/ regular search).

Translation

There [in this list of seven topics], [Vasubandhu] begins by [taking up] the lakṣaṇa-topic and says:

§2 — Kārikā MAV I.1 VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (MS + italic for cd-pāda)

abhūtaparikalpo 'sti dvayan tatra na vidyate |
śūnyatā vidyate tv atra tasyām api sa vidyate || (OCR 1452–1453)

Tibetan (canonical kārikā)

ཡང་དག་མ་ཡིན་ཀུན་རྟོག་ཡོད། ། དེ་ལ་གཉིས་པོ་ཡོད་མ་ཡིན། ། སྟོང་པ་ཉིད་ནི་འདི་ལ་ཡོད། ། དེ་ལ་ཡང་ནི་དེ་ཡོད་དོ། ། (BO_T06_D4032:193b-12)

No change

MAV I.1 is canonically attested at SA_T06_vmvkbh_u:16. The cd-pāda śūnyatā vidyate tv atra tasyām api sa vidyate is independently confirmed by Nagao 1964, Yamaguchi 1934 own-Tibetan witness, Tucci/Bhattacharya 1928. No issues.

Translation

The abhūta-parikalpa (false imagination) exists; in it the duality is not found. Emptiness, however, IS found in it; and in that [emptiness], it [the abhūta-parikalpa] is also found.

§3 — tatra = sapta-bhāva-nirdiṣṭeṣu VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (MS)

iti | tatreti lakṣaṇāvaraṇādi-sapta-bhāva-nirdiṣṭeṣu lakṣaṇam adhikṛtyoddiśyāha | (OCR 1454–1455)

Substantive finding: sapta-bhāva is Sthiramati's deliberate choice

The Tibetan dngos po bdun ("seven entities") unambiguously translates Skt bhāva, not artha. Although MAVBh has ity ete saptārthā, Sthiramati's ṭīkā substitutes sapta-bhāva throughout (cf. recurrence at BO_T06_D4032:232a-26 later in MAVT ch. 1). The MS reading is to be retained; a footnote noting the divergence from MAVBh's saptārtha is appropriate.

[Detailed analysis in mavt-ch1-commentary.html § 3.]

Translation

"iti" [closes the kārikā]. By "tatra" is meant: among the seven entities (sapta-bhāva) set forth — lakṣaṇa, āvaraṇa and so on — having taken up the lakṣaṇa-topic, [Vasubandhu] announces and [glosses] it.

§4 — yathoddeśas tathā nirdeśaḥ meta-paribhāṣā VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (MS)

abhūtaparikalpo 'sti ityādi | yathoddeśas tathā nirdeśa iti kṛtvā lakṣaṇam ca prāg uddiṣṭam atas tasyaiva śeṣebhyo nirdeśaḥ prāg ārabhyate | (OCR 1456–1458)

Substantive finding: Tibetan omits this sentence

The Tibetan MAVT skips this sentence entirely: BO_T06_D4032 moves from :193b-15 directly to :193b-28 with no rendering of yathā-uddeśas tathā nirdeśaḥ. Two possibilities: (a) translators consciously elided a Pāṇinian paribhāṣā that doesn't travel well into Tibetan; (b) translators' exemplar lacked this sentence (i.e. it would be a Sthiramati-MS-only addition). Flag as Tib. om. in the apparatus.

[Full discussion in mavt-ch1-commentary.html § 4.]

Translation

"abhūtaparikalpo 'sti" and so on. Following the [Pāṇinian] maxim "the exposition follows the order of the outline" (yathā-uddeśas tathā nirdeśaḥ): since the lakṣaṇa was outlined first [in the piṇḍārtha], its [detailed] exposition is begun first, before [that of] the remaining [topics].

§5 — refutation of sarvāpavāda: ke cid virundhanti VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (italic)

ke cid virundhanti — sarvadharmāḥ sarvathā niḥsvabhāvāḥ śaśaviṣāṇavad ity — ataḥ sarvāpavāda-pratiṣedhārtham āha — abhūtaparikalpo 'sti (OCR 1459–1461)

Tibetan

ལ་ལ་དག་ཆོས་ཐམས་ཅད་རི་བོང་གི་རྭ་ལྟར་རང་བཞིན་ཡེ་མེད་དོ། ། སྙམ་དུ་སེམས་པ་དེའི་ཕྱིར་ཐམས་ཅད་ལ་སྐུར་པ་དགག་པའི་དོན་དུ། ཡང་དག་མ་ཡིན་ཀུན་རྟོག་ཡོད། ། ཅེས་བྱ་བར་གསུངས་ཏེ། (BO_T06_D4032:193b-26)

No change

The reconstruction is grammatically and doctrinally tight: opening ke cid virundhanti ("some opponents object") + quotation of the Mādhyamika-style nihilist position + ataḥ sarvāpavāda-pratiṣedhārtham āha + pratīka. Tib confirms verbatim: la la dag = ke cid; chos thams cad ri bong gi rwa ltar rang bzhin ye med = sarvadharmāḥ sarvathā niḥsvabhāvāḥ śaśaviṣāṇavat; thams cad la skur pa dgag pa'i don du = sarvāpavāda-pratiṣedhārtham.

Translation

Some object: "All dharmas are utterly without intrinsic nature, like the horn of a hare!" — hence, for the sake of refuting this universal-denial view, [Vasubandhu] says: "The abhūta-parikalpa exists".

§6 — svabhāvataḥ is the implicit qualifier + sūtra-virodha objection VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (MS + bracketed)

[iti |] svabhāvata [Tib. 23, a] iti vākyaśeṣaḥ | nanv evaṁ sūtra-virodhaḥ sarvadharmāḥ śūnyā iti sūtre vacanāt | nāsti virodhaḥ(1) yasmād — dvayam tatra na vidyate | (OCR 1462–1465)

No change

The implicit qualifier svabhāvataḥ ("by intrinsic nature") on asti ("exists") is Sthiramati's standard hermeneutic move — the abhūta-parikalpa "exists by intrinsic nature" (as opposed to "exists conventionally"). The sūtra-virodha objection ("all dharmas are empty, so how can anything exist?") and its anticipatory reply (dvayam tatra na vidyate: the abhūta-parikalpa is "empty" specifically of grāhya-grāhaka) is the standard Yogācāra distinction.

[Full discussion in mavt-ch1-commentary.html § 6.]

Translation

"[iti]". "By intrinsic nature" is the implicit completion [of the verb asti]. — Objection: "But this contradicts the sūtra, since the sūtra teaches that all dharmas are empty!" — [Reply:] There is no contradiction. For: "in it, the duality is not found".

§7 — abhūta-parikalpa as grāhya-grāhaka-svarūpa-rahita VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (italic)

abhūtaparikalpo hi grāhya-grāhaka-svarūpa-rahitaḥ śūnya ucyate, na tu sarvathā niḥsvabhāvaḥ | ato na sūtra-virodhaḥ | (OCR 1466–1467)

Tibetan

ཡང་དག་པ་མ་ཡིན་པ་ཀུན་རྟོག་པ་ནི་གཟུང་བ་དང་འཛིན་པའི་ངོ་བོ་མེད་པས་སྟོང་པ་ཞེས་བརྗོད་ཀྱི། ཐམས་ཅད་དུ་རང་བཞིན་མེད་པ་ནི་མ་ཡིན་ཏེ། དེའི་ཕྱིར་མདོ་དང་མི་འགལ་ལོ། ། (BO_T06_D4032:194a-0)

No change

A clean two-part formulation. Tib gzung ba dang 'dzin pa'i ngo bo med pas = grāhya-grāhaka-svarūpa-rahitaḥ; thams cad du rang bzhin med pa ni ma yin te = na tu sarvathā niḥsvabhāvaḥ; de'i phyir mdo dang mi 'gal lo = ato na sūtra-virodhaḥ. The reconstruction is verbatim.

Translation

For the abhūta-parikalpa is called "empty" in the sense of being devoid of the natures grasped-and-grasper — not in the sense that it is utterly without intrinsic nature. Hence there is no contradiction with the sūtra.

§8 — anticipating the śūnyatā-abhāva-prasaṅga; pointer to MAV I.1c VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (italic + MS)

yady evaṃ dvayam śaśaviṣāṇavat sarvathā nāsti, abhūtaparikalpaś ca paramārthataḥ svabhāvato 'sti, evaṃ śūnyatā-abhāva-prasaṅgaḥ | nāitad evam yasmāc [→ p. 11] (OCR 1467–1470)

No change

A logically clean anticipation of the opponent's next move (if duality is utterly non-existent and abhūta-parikalpa exists svabhāvataḥ, then śūnyatā ceases to be a distinct topic — śūnyatā-abhāva-prasaṅga). The reply is deferred to the next kārikā-pāda (śūnyatā vidyate tv atra) treated on page 11. Tib (194a-0 → 194a-5) confirms the same dialectical structure.

[Full discussion in mavt-ch1-commentary.html § 8.]

Translation

"If indeed the duality is utterly non-existent like a hare's horn, and the abhūta-parikalpa exists ultimately by intrinsic nature — then the absurd consequence follows that śūnyatā [as a separate topic] is non-existent!" — Not so; for [the kārikā continues with the answer] … [→ p. 11]
