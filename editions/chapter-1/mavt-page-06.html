Sthiramati, Madhyāntavibhāgaṭīkā — Yamaguchi p. 6

OCR lines 1320–1353. Methodology: mavt-reedition-workflow.md. Tibetan witness: BO_T06_D4032:191b-13 → 192a-18.

Page 6 is the gloss on the bhāṣya's first sub-section heading, tatrāditaḥ śāstra-śarīraṁ vyavasthāpyate ("there, at the outset, the body of the śāstra is set forth"). Sthiramati unpacks tatra, āditas, śāstra, tac-charīram, vyavasthāpanam, then introduces a small dialectical objection ("if the body is known through the śāstra itself, why introduce it beforehand?") and answers it with a horse-charge simile, then turns to the bhāṣya's saptārtha enumeration: gloss of ete, sapta, arthāḥ, asmiṃ śāstre, uddiṣṭāḥ; finally introduces the lakṣaṇa topic.

The page is mixed: the lemma-citation gloss-pairs are mostly italic (Yamaguchi reconstructions), while the substantive explanatory sentences are MS-attested.

§1 — pratīka tatrāditaḥ śāstra-śarīraṁ vyavasthāpyate + opening gloss PROPOSED

Yamaguchi text

iti | tatrāditaḥ śāstrāśarīravyavasthāpanam iti tatreti śāstrārtha-vivecane śāstre vā | (OCR 1323–1324)

Tibetan

དེ་ལ་ཐོག་མ་ཁོ་ནར་བསྟན་བཅོས་ཀྱི་ལུས་རྣམ་པར་གཞག་སྟེ་ཞེས་བྱ་བ་ལ། དེ་ལ་ཞེས་བྱ་བ་ནི་བསྟན་བཅོས་ཀྱི་དོན་རྣམ་པར་འབྱེད་པ་དེ་ལའམ། བསྟན་བཅོས་ལའོ། ། (BO_T06_D4032:191b-13 → 191b-17)

Restore the MAVBh pratīka form

The OCR reads tatrāditaḥ śāstrāśarīravyavasthāpanam with two issues: (i) the strange long -ā in śāstrā- (should be either śāstra- in a compound or śāstrasya as gen.); (ii) the form vyavasthāpana- as a noun, vs. the MAVBh pratīka vyavasthāpyate (verb). The clean reading is either:
  • Strict pratīka form (matching MAVBh SA_T06_vmvkbh_u:6): tatrāditaḥ śāstra-śarīraṁ vyavasthāpyate iti
  • Sthiramati's nominalisation: tatrāditaḥ śāstra-śarīra-vyavasthāpanam iti

Tib de la thog ma kho nar bstan bcos kyi lus rnam par gzhag ste uses the verbal form rnam par gzhag ste (vyavasthāpyate); MAVBh has the verb. Read the verbal form.

Proposed reading

iti | tatrāditaḥ śāstra-śarīraṁ vyavasthāpyate iti — tatreti śāstra-artha-vivecane śāstre vā |

Translation

"[End of the homage-verse,] 'iti'. [The bhāṣya now reads:] 'There, at the outset, the body of the śāstra is set forth'. — 'tatra' [here means] either in the discrimination of the śāstra's meaning, or in the śāstra [itself].

§3–§4 — śāstra = vivaraṇa; tac-charīram = saṃkṣepaḥ PROPOSED

Yamaguchi text (italic + MS)

śāstram vivaraṇam | tac-charīram saṃkṣepaḥ piṇḍārtho vācrayārthena vā śarīram | (OCR 1324–1325)

Tibetan

བསྟན་བཅོས་ནི་བཤད་པར་ཟད་དོ། ། དེའི་ལུས་ནི་མདོར་བསྡུས་པའམ། དོན་བསྡུས་པའོ། ། གནས་ཀྱི་དོན་ནི་ལུས་སོ། ། (BO_T06_D4032:191b-17 → 191b-22)

One OCR fix: vācrayārthena → vā āśrayārthena

The OCR's vācrayārthena is vā + āśrayārthena with sandhi (vā āśrayārthena → vā ācrayārthena → vācrayārthena) and the OCR-typical ś/c confusion. Tib gnas kyi don ni lus so ("the meaning [as] support/basis is the body") confirms āśraya-arthena. Read vā āśrayārthena.

Other glosses are clean: śāstram = vivaraṇam matches Tib bstan bcos ni bshad par zad do (vivaraṇa = bshad pa, literally "exposition"); saṃkṣepaḥ piṇḍārthaḥ matches Tib mdor bsdus pa'am don bsdus pa ("abbreviated, condensed-meaning").

Proposed reading

śāstraṁ vivaraṇam | tac-charīraṁ saṃkṣepaḥ piṇḍārtho vā āśrayārthena vā śarīram |

Translation

"śāstra" [means] "explanation"; "its body" [means] either the abbreviated form, the condensed-meaning, or [the meaning of] "body" taken in the sense of "support".

§5–§7 — body-analogy + the seven topics VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (MS)

yathā hi bāhya-ādhyātmikāyatanāśrayaḥ kāyaḥ [Tib. 21, a] śarīram ity ucyate | evaṁ yān arthān niśritya śāstraṁ pravartate te 'rthās tasya śarīram | te ca saptārthā lakṣaṇādayaḥ | (OCR 1326–1328)

No change

Clean MS analogy. Tib at 191b-22 matches verbatim: dper na phyi dang nang gi skye mched kyi gnas ni khog pa ste, lus zhes bya ba de bzhin du, don gang dag la gnas te, bstan bcos la 'jug pa'i don de rnams ni de'i lus so | de dag kyang don bdun po dag ste, mtshan nyid la sogs pa dag go.

Translation

Just as the body (kāya) — which is the locus of the inner and outer sense-bases — is called "śarīra", so the topics on the basis of which the śāstra unfolds are its body. And these are the seven topics, [namely] lakṣaṇa and so on.

§8–§9 — vyavasthāpanam = prajñapti / ākhyāna VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text

རྣམ་པར་གཞག་ཅེས་བྱ་བ་ནི་བཅས་པ་སྟེ། བཤད་ཅེས་བྱ་བའི་ཐ་ཚིག་གོ། ། (BO_T06_D4032:191b-28)

§10–§11 — objection (vyavasthāpana redundant?) + horse-charge answer VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (italic objection + MS answer)

nanu śāstra-avabodhenaiva tac charīraṁ vijñāsyata iti nirarthakam ādau tad-vyavasthāpanam | na nirarthakaṁ śiṣyānugrahārthatvād — gatārtho hi śiṣyaḥ sukhaṁ vistaram ucyamānaṁ pratipadyate | dṛṣṭā bhūmiḥ niḥśaṅkaṁ aśvavāhanam iva nānyatheti |(1) (OCR 1329–1333)

n. 1: MS niḥśaṅkaṃ svavāda…, Tib rta thags thogs med par rgyug pa bzhin te = "like a horse running without entangled reins".

Tibetan

བསྟན་བཅོས་ཁོང་དུ་ཆུད་པ་ཉིད་ཀྱིས་དེའི་ལུས་ཤེས་པར་མི་འགྱུར་རམ། … སློབ་མ་རྣམས་ལ་ཕན་གདགས་པའི་ཕྱིར་དོན་མེད་པ་མ་ཡིན་ནོ། ། སློབ་མས་དོན་ཁོང་དུ་ཆུད་ན་རྒྱས་པར་བརྗོད་ཀྱང་བདེ་བླག་ཏུ་ཁོང་དུ་ཆུད་པར་འགྱུར་ཏེ་དཀྱུས་ཀྱིས་བསྟན་པའི་རྟ་ཐགས་ཐོགས་མེད་པར་རྒྱུག་པ་བཞིན་ཏེ། (BO_T06_D4032:191b-28 → 192a-2)

One micro-note on the simile

The MS-attested form aśvavāhanam ("horse-charge / horse-riding") is well-supported by Tib rta thags thogs med par rgyug pa bzhin te ("like a horse running without entangled reins") — Yamaguchi's apparatus n. 1 quotes the same Tibetan. The MS variant svavāda that Yamaguchi notes is just a copyist's mis-transcription.

Translation

"Surely [the body of the śāstra] will be known by [the reader's] comprehension of the śāstra itself — so the prior setting-forth [of the body] is purposeless?" — [Reply:] Not so; it serves to benefit the disciple. For when the disciple has already understood [the outline] meaning, he easily takes in the extensive [later] exposition — like a horse that, on a well-known terrain, runs without hesitation. Not otherwise.

§12–§14 — saptārthā closure + gloss of ete, sapta, arthāḥ, asmin śāstre, uddiṣṭāḥ VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (italic + MS)

ete saptārthā asmiñ śāstra uddiṣṭā iti śāstra-śarīraṁ samāptam ity arthaḥ |(2) eta iti ye lakṣaṇādaya uddiṣṭāḥ | sapteti saṃkhyā paryanta-adhigamārtham upādānam | arthyanta ity arthā adhigamyanta iti yāvat | asmiñ śāstra iti Madhyāntavibhāga-saṁjñake | uddiṣṭā ity upadiṣṭā viniścitā vā | (OCR 1334–1337)

n. 2: MS samāptyarthaḥ, Tib rdzogs so zhes bya ba'i tha tshig go = "the sense is 'it is finished'". n. 3: the paryanta-adhigamārtham upādānam phrase is not in the Tibetan.

No change

The italicised opening ete saptārthā asmiñ śāstra uddiṣṭā is itself the MAVBh pratīka. Yamaguchi's reading samāptam ity arthaḥ matches Tib n. 2 rdzogs so zhes bya ba'i tha tshig go (samāptam iti tatra arthaḥ). The gloss-chain eta / sapta / arthāḥ / asmiñ śāstre / uddiṣṭāḥ is a clean Pāṇinian-style enumeration matching Tib at 192a-4 → 192a-9 verbatim.

Translation

"ete saptārthā asmiñ śāstra uddiṣṭāḥ" — i.e. the body of the śāstra is [thus] complete. "ete" — those lakṣaṇa-and-so-on which have been laid out; "sapta" — a number, adduced to circumscribe the enumeration; "arthāḥ" — they are sought-after, i.e. they are attained; "asmiñ śāstre" — in [the text] called Madhyāntavibhāga; "uddiṣṭāḥ" — taught, or determined.

§15–§17 — turning to lakṣaṇam; twofold lakṣaṇa VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (italic glosses + MS substance)

tathā hīti tad-artha-pratipādana-nipātaḥ | lakṣaṇam iti lakṣyante 'neneti lakṣaṇam | tac ca dvividhaṁ saṁkleśa-lakṣaṇaṁ vyavadāna-lakṣaṇaṁ ca | tatra saṁkleśa-lakṣaṇaṁ nava-vidham | (OCR 1338–1340)

Tibetan

མཚན་ཉིད་ཅེས་བྱ་བ་ནི། འདིས་མཚོན་པར་བྱེད་པས་མཚན་ཉིད་དོ། ། དེ་ཡང་རྣམ་པ་གཉིས་ཏེ། ཀུན་ནས་ཉོན་མོངས་པའི་མཚན་ཉིད་དང་། རྣམ་པར་བྱང་བའི་མཚན་ཉིད་དོ། ། (BO_T06_D4032:192a-9 → 192a-13)

No change

Clean. lakṣyante 'neneti lakṣaṇam ("they are characterized by it — hence lakṣaṇa") = 'dis mtshon par byed pas mtshan nyid do. The twofold split into saṁkleśa-lakṣaṇam / vyavadāna-lakṣaṇam matches Tib kun nas nyon mongs pa'i mtshan nyid / rnam par byang ba'i mtshan nyid.

Translation

"tathā hi" is a particle that introduces the [argument for] that [foregoing] meaning. "lakṣaṇam" — "by which they are characterized, that is the lakṣaṇa (defining feature)". And this is twofold: the saṁkleśa-lakṣaṇa and the vyavadāna-lakṣaṇa. Of these, the saṁkleśa-lakṣaṇa is ninefold.

"Beginning with 'abhūtaparikalpo 'sti' as far as 'saptadhā-bhūta-kalpanāt'" — so the bhāṣya [demarcates the saṁkleśa-lakṣaṇa section]. By the remaining half [of the chapter, MAV I.12 onwards], the vyavadāna-lakṣaṇa is expounded.

§20 — preventing a category-confusion VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (italic + MS)

yadi lakṣyante 'neneti lakṣaṇam ity evaṁ sati lakṣaṇasya saṁkleśa-vyavadāna-bhedo bhaved iti nāitad evam | svabhāva eva hi bhāvānām [→ p. 7] (OCR 1344–1346)

No change

A short anticipatory objection (if lakṣaṇa = "that by which they are characterized", then the lakṣaṇa itself should be subject to the saṁkleśa/vyavadāna distinction; reply: no, this is not so) — completed on p. 7 with the substantive svabhāva-based answer.

Translation

"If lakṣaṇa means 'that by which they are characterized', then [the same word] would itself admit of the saṁkleśa- vs. vyavadāna- distinction" — this is not so: for the very nature of phenomena [is …] [→ p. 7].
