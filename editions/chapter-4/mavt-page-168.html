§24 — Na dauṣṭhulyam akarmaṇyatā iti apare (counter-view) VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (MS-attested)

na dāuṣṭhulyam akarmaṇyatā iti apare | kiṁ kāraṇam | akarmaṇyatā sāsravatvād avaśyaṃ sāsravatā bīja-kāraṇotpannā abhyupagantavyā | sā tu abījā api arhat-saṁtāne kadā cid āhāra-ādy-apacārād bhavati | na ca asāu dāuṣṭhulyam bhavitum arhati anāsravatvād iti | (p. 168; OCR lines 6878–6882)

Structure

The counter-view to §23. "Others again [hold]: dauṣṭhulya is not akarmaṇyatā. [Reasoning:] akarmaṇyatā presupposes sāsrava-tā, and sāsrava-tā must be admitted as arising from a seed-cause. But [akarmaṇyatā] occurs even without a seed in an arhat's continuum, e.g. from food-disturbance (āhārādy-apacāra); and that [arhat-akarmaṇyatā] cannot be dauṣṭhulya, because [the arhat is] anāsrava."

Yamaguchi's Japanese translation

Translation

"[Yet others say:] dauṣṭhulya is not akarmaṇyatā. Why? Because akarmaṇyatā, in virtue of being sāsrava, must necessarily be admitted as sāsrava-state arising from a seed-cause. §25 — Ataś ca sāsrava-dharma-bījam ālaya-vijñāna-sanniviṣṭam dauṣṭhulyena iṣyate VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (MS-attested)

ataś ca sāsrava-dharma-bījam ālaya-vijñāna-sanniviṣṭaṁ dauṣṭhulyena atra iṣyate [Tib. 108, a] | (p. 168; OCR lines 6884–6885)

Structure — a substantial doctrinal point

Sthiramati settles the §23–§24 debate with a Yogācāra-specific definition: "dauṣṭhulya, here [in this MAVbh context], is understood as the seed of sāsrava dharmas deposited in the ālaya-vijñāna." This is a clear ālayavijñāna-based reading of the term dauṣṭhulya — a Yogācāra-Asaṅga / Yogācārabhūmi specification.

Doctrinal parallel

Yogācārabhūmi (Saṃāhitabhūmi etc.) The identification of dauṣṭhulya with the ālaya-vijñāna-deposited seeds of sāsrava-dharmas is a core Yogācāra position. See Yamaguchi's Japanese footnote (mavt-ja line 3827, citing Yogācārabhūmi vol. 2, T 30.284) where the seed-in-ālaya reading is presented as the third interpretation of dauṣṭhulya.

§27 — Tasmāt sukhādy-avakāśa-sahitena kāyena dauṣṭhulyaṃ prabhāvyate (seed-simile) VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text — with another OCR correction

tasmāt sukhādy-avakāśa-sahitena kāyena pratikṣaṇa-bhāvinā tad dauṣṭhulyaṃ prabhāvyate | pratīyata ity arthaḥ | tadyathā khalavila-antargataṃ bījam aṅkureṇa iti | tasmāt kāya-parīkṣayā duḥkha-satyam avatarati | (p. 168; OCR lines 6886–6889, reading avakāśa for OCR avakāca)

OCR-correction note

OCR avakāca is a misread of Yamaguchi's printed avakāśa ("opportunity, occasion") — Devanagari śa (श) misread as ca (च). Confirmed by the corpus-indexed Yamaguchi text (SA_T06_sthmavtyg:3396); sense supported by Yaśomitra's Abhidharmasamuccaya-vyākhyā BO_S05_NGONPA-109:8808, which renders the equivalent avakāśa-sense with Tibetan skabs su ("on the occasions of") in a parallel context.

Structure

Sthiramati's gloss + simile: "Therefore, dauṣṭhulya is made manifest by the body, [a body that is] moment-to-moment-arising and accompanied by the occasions [for the arising] of pleasure etc. — [prabhāvyate] means [dauṣṭhulya] is inferred. Just as the seed enclosed in a granary-bin is [inferred] by the sprout. Therefore, by examining the body, [the practitioner] enters the truth of suffering."

The bīja in khalavila + aṅkura simile (seed-in-granary-bin inferred by sprout) is a Yogācāra signature analogy for the ālaya-vijñāna's hidden seeds being inferred through their manifest effects.

Doctrinal parallel

Yaśomitra, Abhidharmasamuccayavyākhyā — BO_S05_NGONPA-109:8808. The same Sthiramati-style argument is glossed in Tibetan (skabs su / skad cig pas khyab po) — confirms the "occasion of arising" reading for avakāśa here.

§29 — Sa-bīja-saṃskārātmakatvād ity arthaḥ … VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (MS-attested)

sa-bīja-saṃskārātmakatvād ity arthaḥ | saṃskāra-duḥkhatā hi vipariṇāma-duḥkhatā-duḥkha-duḥkhatā- dauṣṭhulyābhyām anugatatvāt [tat-] kāraṇatvāc ca dauṣṭhulyam ity uktā | ata eva saṃskāra-duḥkhatayā sarva-sāsravaṃ vastv āryair duḥkhatvenālokitam | (p. 168; OCR lines 6891–6895, with Yamaguchi fns. 3–4)

Apparatus notes (Yamaguchi fns. 3, 4, 5)

  • fn. 3: MS reads …ekatvād; Tib. bdag-ñid yin-paḥi phyir — supports (saṃskāra)-ātmakatvāt.
  • fn. 4: Tib. deḥi rgyu yin-paḥi phyir — supports inserting tat- in tat-kāraṇatvāc.
  • fn. 5: Tib. ḥdu-byed-kyi sdug-bsṅal (= saṃskāra-duḥkhatā) — confirms the closing reading.

Structure

Sthiramati glosses the MAVbh pratīka of §28: "sa-bīja- saṃskāra-ātmakatvāt" — i.e. "[duḥkha-satya is dauṣṭhulya-saṃskāra] because [duḥkha-satya] is the nature of saṃskāras with seeds." Then the two-fold justification of calling saṃskāra-duḥkhatā dauṣṭhulya: (i) it pervades the other two duḥkhatās with their dauṣṭhulya; (ii) it is their cause. Closes with the standard formula: "And that is precisely why all defiled things are seen by the noble ones as having the nature of suffering, [viz.] through saṃskāra-duḥkhatā."

§31 — Tṛṣṇā-hetur vedanā (opens vedanā-smṛtyupasthāna, continues to p. 207) VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text — opens vedanā section

tṛṣṇā-hetur vedanā | avidyā-saṃsparśa-jā iti abhiprāyāt | anāsravāyāḥ ataddhetutvāt | tatra sukhā vedanā tat-saṃyoga- aviyoga-… (p. 168 → 169; OCR lines 6896–6898, continues at top of p. 207)

Direct MAVbh quotation

Vasubandhu MAVbh ad IV.1 — local references/SA_T06_vmvkbh_u.txt line 811; indexed in SA_T06_vmvkbh_u. tṛṣṇāhetur vedanā tatparīkṣayā samudayasatyam avatarati / "Vedanā is the cause of tṛṣṇā; by examining it, [the practitioner] enters the truth of origin." Sthiramati lifts the first phrase tṛṣṇā-hetur vedanā verbatim as a pratīka and proceeds to gloss it.

Structure (opens vedanā→samudaya section)

Sthiramati's gloss: "tṛṣṇā-hetur vedanā [is meant in a restricted sense:] the implication being [the vedanā] born of contact-with-ignorance (avidyā-saṃsparśa-jā) — because [the vedanā] of the anāsrava [arhat] is not a cause of that [tṛṣṇā]. Among these, pleasant vedanā [is the cause] of the craving-for-non-separation-from-it…" [continues on page 207 with the three-fold breakdown of how each kind of vedanā causes a specific kind of tṛṣṇā].

Yamaguchi vs. proposed (no change)

Yamaguchi 1934 as printed
tṛṣṇāhetur vedanā | avidyāsaṁsparçajety abhiprāyāt | anāsravāyā ataddhetutvāt | tatra sukhā vedanā tatsaṁyogāviyoga-…
Proposed re-edited text
"tṛṣṇā-hetur vedanā" | avidyā-saṃsparśa-jā iti abhiprāyāt | anāsravāyāḥ a-tad-dhetutvāt | tatra sukhā vedanā tat-saṃyoga- aviyoga-… [continues p. 207]

Yamaguchi's Japanese translation

渇愛の因は受なりと云ふは、無明の觸より生ぜる〔受〕との意趣なる故に、無漏なる〔受〕はそれの因に非るが故なり。その中、樂受はそれと和合して離れざらんとの渇愛の因なり。 (references/mavt-ja.txt lines 3791–3792)

Translation

"'Vedanā is the cause of craving' [is to be understood restrictedly]: the implication being [the vedanā] born of contact-with-ignorance (avidyā-saṃsparśa-jā) — because the vedanā of an anāsrava [arhat] is not a cause of [craving]. Among these, pleasant vedanā is the cause of [craving for] non-separation from it…"
