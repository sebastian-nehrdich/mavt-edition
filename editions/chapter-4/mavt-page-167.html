📝 noteThe Madhyāntavibhāgabhāṣya (Vasubandhu) gives here not only the word tAvat "first", but also Adau "in the beginning", which carries more or less the same meaning. Thus the use of these almost synonymous words has an emphatic effect at this place. The word tAvat can also, but less frequently, be rendered not as "first", but as "clearly", which at this point would give emphasis to the following Adau. Following this understanding, a translation of these two words in the manner of "clearly, first" would appear to be possible. However Sthiramati's gloss of the word tAvat at this point contradicts such an interpretation.
