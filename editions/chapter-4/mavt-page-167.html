§10 — Iti vistaraḥ | tatreti bodhipakṣa-bhāvanāyām | tāvac-chabdaḥ kramārthaḥ VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text

iti vistaraḥ | tatreti bodhipakṣabhāvanāyām | tāvacchabdaḥ kramārthaḥ | (p. 167; OCR line 6838)

Structure

Three brisk glosses closing the kārikā IV.1a quotation:

  • iti vistaraḥ — "[so the kārikā goes on] in full" — closes the abbreviated citation begun at §9. Standard MAVT citation-closing formula (parallel at OCR line 6816 of §7 context).
  • tatra iti bodhipakṣa-bhāvanāyām — glosses tatra from the kārikā / bhāṣya lead-in (tatra tāvad ādau): "by 'tatra' is meant 'in the cultivation-of-bodhipakṣya.'" Pratīka + locative gloss.
  • tāvac-chabdaḥ krama-arthaḥ — "the word tāvat has the meaning 'in order'/'in sequence'" — i.e. tāvat is a sequence-marker, signalling that smṛtyupasthāna-bhāvanā is treated first in the sequence of bodhipakṣa-bhāvanās.

§12 — Nirvāṇa-saṃsārayor guṇadoṣānavabodhāt … VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (MS-attested, with two MS-vs-Tib variants noted by Yamaguchi)

nirvāṇasaṁsārayor guṇadoṣānavabodhāt saṁskāreṣu sukhaśucinityātmābhiniveśena bālānāṁ bhavabhoge 'bhimandanaṁ nirvāṇārthe trāsaḥ | sa ca guṇadoṣānavabodhaḥ sukhādyabhiniveśaś ca satyadarśanād vinivartata ity ataḥ saṁsārād udvejanārthaṁ nirvāṇe cābhimukhīkaraṇārthaṁ catuḥsatyāvatārāya smṛtyupasthānabhāvanaivādāv uktā | (p. 167; OCR lines 6840–6845)

Apparatus notes from Yamaguchi (footnotes 1, 2, 3)

  • fn. 1: MS reads nivārṇa; Tib. mya-ṅan-las ḥdas-pa — so nirvāṇa is the correct reading (MS is a metathesis slip).
  • fn. 2: MS reads vacanaṁ nivāṇa- (perhaps an inserted vacanam that should be removed); Tib. mñon-par dgaḥ-ba çiṅ mya-ṅan-las-ḥdas-pa — supports 'bhimandanaṁ nirvāṇa (gerundive of abhi-mand "delighting in").
  • fn. 3: In Vasubandhu's MAVbh, this very sentence appears at the end of the smṛtyupasthāna section, and where Sthiramati has uktā, MAVbh has vyavasthāpyate. Cf. local MAVbh file line 815: ādau catuḥsatyāvatārāya smṛtyupasthānabhāvanā vyavasthāpyate /

The MAVbh parallel — Sthiramati anticipates Vasubandhu's prose

Vasubandhu MAVbh ad IV.1 closing — local references/SA_T06_vmvkbh_u.txt line 815; indexed in SA_T06_vmvkbh_u. ādau catuḥsatyāvatārāya smṛtyupasthānabhāvanā vyavasthāpyate / Sthiramati lifts this MAVbh formula but substitutes the perfect passive uktā for the present vyavasthāpyate — because in his commentarial order this statement falls at the beginning (anticipating what Vasubandhu will say), not at the end.

Yamaguchi's Japanese translation

諸愚昧者、涅槃と生死との功徳と過失とを悟了せざる由り、諸行中樂淨常我を執著するによりて有と資具とを喜愛し、涅槃の爲には恐怖あり。然るに彼功徳と過失との不了悟と樂等に對する執著とは諦を見ることによりて止息す。されば輪廻を厭離することを目的とし、及び、涅槃に現向することを目的として四諦に入る為に、念の修習をこそ初めに説きたるなり。 (references/mavt-ja.txt lines 3749–3751)

Translation

"Because ordinary people [bāla] fail to comprehend the virtues and defects of nirvāṇa and saṃsāra, and through their attachment to the saṃskāras as pleasant, pure, permanent, and self-possessing, they delight in mundane enjoyment and dread nirvāṇa. But that failure to comprehend the virtues-and-defects, and that attachment to pleasure and so on, are reversed by the seeing of the [noble] truths. Therefore, in order to disenchant [the practitioner] from saṃsāra and to turn [them] toward nirvāṇa, the cultivation of the foundations of mindfulness is taught precisely at the outset, [as the means] for entry into the four [noble] truths."

§13 — Kāyena dauṣṭhulyaṃ prabhāvyate iti — dauṣṭhulyaṃ duḥsthitatā VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (italics = MAVbh pratīka)

kāyena dāuṣṭhulyaṃ prabhāvyate iti dauṣṭhulyaṃ duḥsthitatā | (p. 167; OCR line 6846)

The MAVbh source (verbatim pratīka)

Vasubandhu MAVbh ad IV.1 — local references/SA_T06_vmvkbh_u.txt line 806; indexed in SA_T06_vmvkbh_u. kāyena hi dauṣṭhulyaṃ prabhāvyate / Verbatim MAVbh, with the bhāṣya's hi appropriately omitted in Sthiramati's pratīka (he is quoting the lemma to gloss it).

Structure

Sthiramati quotes the MAVbh sentence kāyena dauṣṭhulyaṃ prabhāvyate ("by [observing] the body, dauṣṭhulya is made manifest") and immediately glosses dauṣṭhulyaṃ duḥsthitatā — "dauṣṭhulya means 'being in a bad state' / 'dys-functionality.'" Standard Yogācāra lexical gloss; the term dauṣṭhulya is a Yogācāra technical term for the bodily-mental disability that the bodhipakṣya-cultivation removes.

§14 — Tathā hi pratidinam abhisaṃskriyamāṇo vikriyate eva kāyaḥ VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (MS-attested)

tathā hi pratidinaṁ abhisaṁskriyamāṇo vikriyata eva kāya iti kāyena dāuṣṭhulyaṁ prabhāvyate | (p. 167; OCR lines 6847–6848)

Structure

Sthiramati's gloss on what it means for the body to manifest dauṣṭhulya: "indeed, the body, being daily made-up (abhi-saṃskriyamāṇa, i.e. groomed/conditioned), is in fact transformed [continually]" — so its very impermanence is what makes dauṣṭhulya apparent to investigation. Sthiramati then re-quotes the MAVbh pratīka kāyena dauṣṭhulyaṃ prabhāvyate as the conclusion drawn from this gloss.

§16 — Tal-lakṣaṇasya hi sadauṣṭhulya-saṃskāratvāt iti VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (italics = reconstructed)

kim-artham etad iti āha | tallakṣaṇasya hi sadauṣṭhulyasaṃskāratvād iti | (p. 167; OCR line 6849–6850)

The MAVbh discrepancy

Yamaguchi's reconstruction differs from Nagao's MAVbh Sanskrit in syntactically substantial way:

Nagao's MAVbh Sanskrit edition — local references/SA_T06_vmvkbh_u.txt line 808; indexed in SA_T06_vmvkbh_u. tasya sadauṣṭhulyasaṃskāra-LAKṢAṆATVĀT / "Because that [body] HAS THE CHARACTERISTIC of being a saṃskāra-with- dauṣṭhulya." — subject is tasya [kāyasya]; predicate is -lakṣaṇatvāt.

The Tibetan evidence — a substantive MAVbh variant

Both Tibetan witnesses are verbatim identical at this lemma, and both support Yamaguchi's reconstruction tallakṣaṇasya … saṃskāratvāt over Nagao's tasya … lakṣaṇatvāt:

Vasubandhu MAVbh-Tib — BO_T06_D4027:15b-26. ལུས་ཀྱིས་ནི་གནས་ངན་ལེན་རབ་ཏུ་སྟོན་ཏེ། དེ་ཡང་ཡོངས་སུ་ བརྟགས་པས་སྡུག་བསྔལ་གྱི་བདེན་པ་ལ་འཇུག་གོ ། དེའི་མཚན་ཉིད་ནི་གནས་ངན་ལེན་དང་བཅས་པའི་འདུ་བྱེད་ཡིན་པའི་ཕྱིར་རོ། The bolded clause translates a Sanskrit tal-lakṣaṇasya … saṃskāratvāt, not tasya … lakṣaṇatvāt.

Verdict

Yamaguchi's italics reconstruction is vindicated. Both Tibetan translations — made independently — attest the same Sanskrit pre-form tal-lakṣaṇasya … saṃskāratvāt. The divergence is therefore at the level of MAVbh Sanskrit transmission: Sthiramati's MAVbh-Vorlage differed from the Sanskrit MS Nagao edited. This is a substantive Sanskrit variant attested by both Tibetan translations against the lone Nagao MS — worth flagging in any future MAVbh apparatus too.
Further internal confirmation from Sthiramati's own gloss in §17: yasmād duḥkhasatyasya sadauṣṭhulyāḥ saṃskārā lakṣaṇaṃ svabhāvaḥ ("because the saṃskāras with dauṣṭhulya are the lakṣaṇa, the nature, of duḥkha-satya") — i.e. Sthiramati's gloss presupposes duḥkha-satya / tal-lakṣaṇa as the genitive subject, and sa-dauṣṭhulya-saṃskāra as the predicate-attribute. Exactly the syntax of tal-lakṣaṇasya … saṃskāratvāt.

§17 — Yasmād duḥkha-satyasya sadauṣṭhulyāḥ saṃskārā lakṣaṇaṃ svabhāvaḥ … VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (MS-attested)

yasmād duḥkha-satyasya sadauṣṭhulyāḥ saṁskārā lakṣaṇaṁ svabhāvaḥ kāyena ca dāuṣṭhulyaṁ prabhāvyate | tasmāt kāya-parīkṣayā duḥkha-satyam avatarati | dāuṣṭhulyaṁ na vijñāyata ity ata āha | (p. 167; OCR lines 6850–6853)

Structure

Sthiramati's yasmāt … tasmāt syllogism. Three steps:

  1. The saṃskāras with dauṣṭhulya are the lakṣaṇa, the nature, of duḥkha-satya (= the substance of §16).
  2. dauṣṭhulya is made manifest by the body (= MAVbh pratīka of §13).
  3. Therefore, entry into duḥkha-satya is by body-investigation (= MAVbh of §15).

The closing dauṣṭhulyaṁ na vijñāyata ity ata āha ("[but] 'dauṣṭhulya is not [yet] known' — for that reason [the bhāṣyakāra] says:") sets up the next MAVbh pratīka in §18 (dauṣṭhulyaṃ hi saṃskāra-duḥkhatā).

Internal confirmation of §16

Note that duḥkha-satyasya sadauṣṭhulyāḥ saṃskārā lakṣaṇaṃ ("the saṃskāras with dauṣṭhulya are the lakṣaṇa of duḥkha-satya") is precisely Sthiramati paraphrasing the syntax of tal-lakṣaṇasya … saṃskāratvāt from §16 — i.e. the subject is duḥkha-satya / tal-lakṣaṇa; the predicate is sa-dauṣṭhulya-saṃskāra. This is decisive internal evidence that Sthiramati's MAVbh-Vorlage had the tal-lakṣaṇasya form (cf. §16 finding).

§19 — Saṃskāro bhāvānām utpattiḥ … yathoktam: anartho janma VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text

saṁskāro bhāvānām utpattiḥ | sa ca sarva-viplava-vastu iti dāuṣṭhulyaṁ āryāṇāṁ pratikūlatvāc ca duḥkhatā | yathoktam — anartho janma idaṃ sati hi janane santi ripavo jarā-mṛtyu-vyādhi-vyasana-vadha-bandha- prabhṛtayaḥ | anarthās te na syur yady avināśo 'nutpādaḥ syāt | vṛkṣe 'nutpanne 'gni-vāyu-vana-agni-bhraṃśo na syād iti | (p. 167; OCR lines 6854–6859)

Structure

Gloss on the MAVbh pratīka of §18 (dauṣṭhulyaṃ hi saṃskāra-duḥkhatā):

  1. saṃskāro bhāvānām utpattiḥ — "saṃskāra is the arising of beings." Mini-definition. The bhāvānām utpattiḥ gloss is italicised — Yamaguchi reconstructs it (perhaps because the MS had only "saṃskāro …" with the rest lost). Confirmed by the Tibetan if any.
  2. sa ca sarva-viplava-vastu — "and it (saṃskāra) is the basis of every disturbance." This is dauṣṭhulya.
  3. … āryāṇāṃ pratikūlatvāc ca duḥkhatā — "and because it is adverse to the noble ones, [it is] duḥkhatā." Hence "saṃskāra-duḥkhatā."
  4. Verse-citation yathoktam — anartho janma …: scriptural support that birth is the root evil.

Verse-citation hunt

Source unidentified. The verse anartho janma idaṃ sati hi janane … vṛkṣe 'nutpanne 'gni-vāyu-vana-agni-bhraṃśo na syād does not appear in the indexed Sanskrit corpus anywhere except in Sthiramati's own MAVT quotation itself (SA_T06_sthmavtyg:3379). None of the obvious candidates (Bodhicaryāvatāra and pañjikā, Aśvaghoṣa's Saundarananda/Buddhacarita, Catuḥśataka, MSABh, YBh) yields the distinctive tree-simile clause vṛkṣe 'nutpanne … bhraṃśo. The yathoktam formula implies a real citation — likely a sūtra excerpt or lost prose-source. Worth chasing in the Tibetan canonical translator-notes (Derge / Peking apparatus) or in older secondary literature on the MAVT.

鏖重は實に行苦なりと。行は諸法の生起なり。そは又一切の害の依處なるが故に鏖重にして、 諸聖人に背戻する故に又苦性なり。 説かれたるが如し、「無常なる生は害なり。所以は生あるときには老、死、病、不幸、殺害、繋縛等の怨敵あればなり。若し無滅無生ならばそれらの害無かるべし。木生じたるに非るときは、火と風と森の火とによる破壊無きが如し」と。 (references/mavt-ja.txt lines 3760–3766)

Translation

"[On the bhāṣya's dauṣṭhulyaṃ hi saṃskāra-duḥkhatā:] saṃskāra is the arising of beings. And it is the basis of every disturbance: thus it is dauṣṭhulya. And, being adverse to the noble ones, it is also duḥkhatā. As it is said: 'Birth is the [root] evil. For when there is birth, there exist enemies — old age, death, disease, calamity, killing, bondage, and so forth. These evils would not occur if non-destruction were non-arising. §20 — Tayeti saṃskāra-duḥkhatayā … (page 205 → 206) VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (italics = MAVbh pratīka)

tayeti saṁskāra-duḥkhatayā | na vipariṇāma-duḥkhatā-duḥkha-duḥkhatābhyām | tayor avyāpitvāt saṁskāra-duḥkha-[tā-nimittatvāc ca …] (p. 167–168; OCR lines 6860–6862, continues on page 206)

The MAVbh source (verbatim)

Vasubandhu MAVbh ad IV.1 — local references/SA_T06_vmvkbh_u.txt line 810; indexed in SA_T06_vmvkbh_u. tayā sarvaṃ sāsravaṃ vastv āryā duḥkhataḥ paśyantīti / The MAVbh has tayā as instrumental of saṃskāra-duḥkhatā: "by it [= by saṃskāra-duḥkhatā], the āryas see all defiled things as duḥkha." Sthiramati's tayeti is a pratīka quoting that tayā and glossing it: "by it means: by saṃskāra-duḥkhatā."

Structure

Sthiramati's gloss: tayā in the MAVbh refers specifically to saṃskāra-duḥkhatā, not to either of the other two duḥkhatās (vipariṇāma-duḥkhatā, duḥkha-duḥkhatā). Reason: the other two are not pervasive (avyāpitva) and saṃskāra-duḥkhatā is the underlying basis (the bottom of page 205 cuts off at saṃskāra-duḥkha-[tā-nimittatvāt], continued p. 206).
