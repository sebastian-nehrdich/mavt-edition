📝 noteThe word 'Sūtra' at this point very likely refers to the Madhyāntavibhāga (Maitreya). In the śāstraśarīra-section at the beginning of the Madhyāntavibhāgaṭīkā (Sthiramati), the text that is commented on is referred to as madhyāntavibhāgasūtrabhāṣya (Madhyāntavibhāgaṭīkā (Yamaguchi 1934) p.1, l. 3).
