Sthiramati, Madhyāntavibhāgaṭīkā — Yamaguchi p. 170 (chapter 4)

Yamaguchi 1934 file p. 208. Chapter 4 (Pratipakṣa-bhāvanā-pariccheda). Edited from Sebastian Nehrdich's TEI-style critical edition; all variants and citations preserved. Sanskrit/Tibetan quotations link to a search on DharmaMitra / DharmaNexus.

Close of dharma-smṛtyupasthāna; the definition of smṛtyupasthāna (mindfulness-faculty vs. wisdom-nature); the four cultivations (impermanence, suffering, emptiness, no-self) + their elaboration for kāya / vedanā / citta / dharma; transition to samyak-prahāṇa. Sentences §48–§53.

English
"Entering" here means understanding the characteristics of the truth within each truth separately. Among these [different types of cultivation] the cultivation of the establishment of mindfulness of the body is [the cultivation of the body]
📝 noteThe term "cultivation" is repeated in the Tibetan and therefore this sense is yielded, even though it is not explicitly expressed this way in the Sanskrit.
