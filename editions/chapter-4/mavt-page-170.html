§40 — Vyāvadānika dharmas = aśubha-maitryādayaḥ VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (italicised reconstruction)

[upakleśāḥ |] bhūtāḥ premamaitryādayaḥ | teṣāṃ sāṃkleśikānāṃ vyāvadānikānāṃ ca vipakṣa-pratipakṣa-bhāvaṃ buddhvā sarva-upadrava-atyanta-prahāṇa-upāye mārga-satye 'bhimukhī-bhavati | evaṃ dharma-smṛtyupasthānena mārga-satyam avatarati iti | (p. 170; OCR lines 6947–6950, with Yamaguchi fn. 1)

Substantive proposed emendation — for fn. 1

Read aśubha-maitryādayaḥ for Yamaguchi's premamaitryādayaḥ. The Tibetan BO_T06_D4032:272a-0 reads mi sdug pa dang byams pa la sogs pa'o — unambiguously aśubha + maitrī + ādi. Yamaguchi's suggestion in fn. 1 that the Tibetan mi "not" is an erroneous addition is methodologically possible but textually unsupported: aśubha-bhāvanā (impurity-contemplation) is the standard vipakṣa of rāga, and pairs canonically with maitrī (counteragent of dveṣa) and pratītyasamutpāda (counteragent of moha) in the three caritānusārin meditations. The MS …nyādayaḥ reflects underlying [aśubha-]maitryādayaḥ with the m lost in a broken folio. This is the strongest single emendation on the page.

それら雑染〔法〕と清浄〔法〕との對治と所對治との相を知り已りて、 一切の害を畢竟じて断ずる方便なる道諦に現向することとなる。 かくの如く法念處によりて道諦に入る。 (references/mavt-ja.txt lines 3812–3813)

Translation

"[The defiling dharmas are kleśas and] upakleśas; [the purifying ones are] aśubha-[contemplation], maitrī, and so on. Having understood the vipakṣa-pratipakṣa relation between those defiling and purifying [dharmas], [the practitioner] becomes inclined toward the truth of the path (mārga-satya), which is the means for the absolute abandonment of all calamities. Thus by means of dharma-smṛtyupasthāna, [the practitioner] enters the truth of the path."

Yamaguchi vs. proposed (no emendation)

Yamaguchi's reconstruction matches the Tibetan exactly. The indexed corpus also returned no Buddhist parallel for this specific 6-aśuci sub-series — the closest analogues (YBh-Śrāvakabhūmi aśubha list, AKBh II.117ff.) use a different 4-fold scheme (bīja / āśraya / svabhāva / parijñāna). Sthiramati's aśuci-bīja → -niṣyanda → -sthiti-hetu → -vikāra → ivāsāra (phena-bhaṅga) → gati/yoni appears to be his own systematisation — worth flagging as a Sthiramati-signature.

Translation

"Avatāra here is the comprehension, with respect to each [noble] truth, of its defining mark. Among these, the cultivation of kāya-smṛtyupasthāna contemplates [the body] as impermanent, suffering, empty, selfless; also as having the nature of multiple impure substances; as arising from impure seed; as impure in its outflow; as having impure sustaining-causes; as [undergoing] impure transformations; as coreless, like a clot of foam; and as [the field of] destinies and wombs."

§42 — Vedanā/citta/dharma 12-fold contemplation + bodhisattva-mode VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (italicised)

vedanā-citta-dharma-smṛtyupasthāna-bhāvanā punar anitya-duḥkha-śūnya-anātmato 'pi sva-lakṣaṇato 'pi hetuto 'pi phalato 'pi dhātu-bhūmi-gatito 'pi kuśala-akuśala- avyākṛtato 'pi vipakṣa-pratipakṣato 'pi | bodhisattvānāṃ punaḥ smṛtyupasthāna-bhāvanā evaṃ ca anupalambha-ākāreṇa ca vikalpa-prahāṇārtham | (p. 170; OCR lines 6956–6960)

Two substantive proposed emendations

(a) Insert sāmānya-lakṣaṇato 'pi between anātmato 'pi and sva-lakṣaṇato 'pi.
The Tibetan BO_T06_D4032:272a-18 reads "… spyi'i mtshan nyid dang rang gi mtshan nyid dang …" — the standard Abhidharma pairing sāmānya-lakṣaṇa / sva-lakṣaṇa. Yamaguchi's Sanskrit list has 8 items where the Tibetan has 9. Confirmed retrospectively by §44's summary at BO_T06_D4032:272b-2 (spyi'i mtshan nyid la sogs pa'i rnam pa). This is precisely the kind of "longer Tibetan" Yamaguchi flagged in fn. 4.
(b) Reverse word order in the bodhisattva sentence. The Tibetan reads: "byang chub sems dpa' rnams kyi dran pa nye bar gzhag pa bsgom pa ni 'di lta bu yang yin la rnam par rtog pa spangs pa'i phyir mi dmigs pa'i rnam par yang bsgom mo" — i.e. "vikalpa-prahāṇa is the cause/reason for the anupalambha-ākāra cultivation", not its purpose as Yamaguchi has it. Read: … evaṃ ca, vikalpa-prahāṇād (or -ārthaṃ) anupalambha-ākāreṇa ca bhāvyate. Yamaguchi's word order with vikalpa-prahāṇārtham at the end as the purpose-clause of the whole sentence inverts the Tibetan's causal logic.

Translation

"The cultivation of vedanā-, citta-, dharma-smṛtyupasthāna contemplates [each] as impermanent, suffering, empty, selfless; also as sāmānya-lakṣaṇa [general characteristic]; also as sva-lakṣaṇa [specific characteristic]; as cause; as effect; as pertaining to dhātu / bhūmi / gati; as wholesome / unwholesome / indeterminate; as vipakṣa / pratipakṣa. §43 — Smṛtyupasthāna-definition + opens samyak-prahāṇa (kārikā IV.2a) VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (italicised)

smṛtyupasthānānāṃ tu prajñāsvābhāvye 'pi smṛtyupasthāna-vacanaṃ smṛti-balena upasthānāt etāsāṃ prajñānāṃ smṛtyupasthānam ucyate |

[b) Catvāri samyakprahāṇāni]

smṛtyupasthāna-bhāvanā-anantaraṃ samyak [Tib. 109, b] prahāṇa-bhāvanā uktā | kiṁ kāraṇam ata āha | yasmāt parijñāte vipakṣe ca [kārikā IV.2a] iti vistaraḥ | (p. 170; OCR lines 6960–6969, with Yamaguchi fnn. 2, 3)

Substantive proposed emendation (against Yamaguchi fn. 2)

Read smṛti-balena upasthānād vā etair (or etābhiḥ) upasthānāt smṛtyupasthānam ucyate for Yamaguchi's smṛti-balena upasthānād etāsāṃ prajñānāṃ smṛtyupasthānam ucyate.
The Tibetan BO_T06_D4032:272a-34 reads "dran pa'i stobs kyis nye bar gnas pa'i phyir ram / 'di dag gis dran pa nye bar gnas pas dran pa nye bar gzhag pa zhes bya'o" — i.e. TWO etymologies joined by the disjunctive ram = Skt. vā: (a) because [the prajñās] are present by the power of smṛti; OR (b) because smṛti is present through them (etair upasthānāt). Yamaguchi's fn. 2 suggested that ram is a scribal error for śes-rab = prajñā — this is wrong. The ram is the disjunctive particle (-ram sandhi for "or"), and the Sanskrit needs vā, not the genitive etāsāṃ prajñānām.

Sthiramati cites only the opening pratīka yasmāt parijñāte vipakṣe ca and gestures past the rest with iti vistaraḥ.

Translation

"Although the smṛtyupasthānas are by their proper nature prajñā, the term smṛtyupasthāna is used [because they are present by the power of smṛti], or [because smṛti takes its stand through them]. Hence [these prajñās] are called smṛtyupasthāna.

[b) The Four samyak-prahāṇas]
Immediately after the cultivation of smṛtyupasthāna, the cultivation of the samyak-prahāṇas is taught. What is the reason? It is stated: '[Kārikā IV.2:] When the vipakṣa and [pratipakṣa] are entirely understood,' [the verse continues] — and so forth."

§44 — Smṛtyupasthāna-bhāvanayā iti + lacuna reconstruction → p. 209 VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (italicised + long lacuna)

smṛtyupasthāna-bhāvanayā iti | aviśeṣa-abhidhāne 'pi ⁽⁴⁾ dharma-smṛtyupasthāna-bhāvanayā eva | iti veditavyam | dhātu-bhū- [long lacuna — continues on p. 209] (p. 170 → 171; OCR lines 6969–6970, fn. 4 flags lacuna)

Significant proposed reconstruction (resolves Yamaguchi fn. 4)

The Tibetan BO_T06_D4032:272b-2 preserves the missing material in full and it reconnects continuously to the surviving Sanskrit on p. 209 line 1 (OCR lines 6981–6984). Yamaguchi's fn. 4 worry that "the underlying Sanskrit may be a different reading from the Tibetan" is not borne out — the Tibetan is simply longer because the MS lost a leaf-edge of material that survives in continuous parallel form in the Tibetan.

Proposed reconstruction for the lacuna:
dhātu-bhū[mi-prahāṇena ca sāmānya-lakṣaṇādy-ākāraiḥ sāsrava-anāsrava-dhātu-bhūmi-śaikṣa-aśaikṣādy-ākāraiḥ tathā sāsrava-anāsrava-dhātu-bhūmi-śaikṣa-aśaikṣādi-bhedena ca vipakṣe pratipakṣe ca sarva-prakāra-parijñāte …]

This yields a continuous text into p. 209 line 1, which already preserves sāsravānāsravadhātubhūmiśaikṣāśaikṣādyākāraiḥ … ca vipakṣe pratipakṣe ca sarvaprakāraparijñāte (OCR lines 6981–6983). The "lecture différente" Yamaguchi worried about dissolves.
