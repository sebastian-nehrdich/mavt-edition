§32 — Three vedanās × three tṛṣṇās VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (mixed)

[sukhā vedanā tat-saṃyoga-aviyoga-] tṛṣṇāyā hetuḥ | duḥkhā tad-asaṃyoga-viyoga-tṛṣṇāyā hetuḥ | aduḥkhāsukhā tad-avasthā-abhirati-tṛṣṇāyāḥ tat-saṃyoga- aviyoga-tṛṣṇāyāś ca kasyāṃ cit samāpattau hetur iti | (p. 169; OCR lines 6908–6910)

Structure

The three-vedanā × three-tṛṣṇā analysis:

  • sukhā vedanā → tṛṣṇā for non-separation (saṃyogāviyoga-tṛṣṇā).
  • duḥkhā vedanā → tṛṣṇā for non-encounter and separation (asaṃyoga-viyoga-tṛṣṇā).
  • aduḥkhāsukhā vedanā → in some samāpatti, a cause of either tṛṣṇā for staying in that state (tadavasthā-abhirati-tṛṣṇā) or tṛṣṇā for non-separation.

Standard Abhidharma teaching, cf. Abhidharmakośa / Yogācārabhūmi.

Yamaguchi vs. proposed (no change)

Yamaguchi 1934 as printed
… tṛṣṇāyā hetuḥ | duḥkhā tadasaṁyogaviyogatṛṣṇāyā hetuḥ | aduḥkhāsukhā tadavasthābhiratitṛṣṇāyās tatsaṁyogāviyogatṛṣṇāyāś ca kasyāṁ cit samāpattau hetur iti |
Proposed re-edited text
[sukhā vedanā tat-saṃyoga-aviyoga-]tṛṣṇāyā hetuḥ | duḥkhā tad-asaṃyoga-viyoga-tṛṣṇāyā hetuḥ | aduḥkhāsukhā tad-avasthā-abhirati-tṛṣṇāyāḥ tat-saṃyoga-aviyoga-tṛṣṇāyāś ca kasyāṃ cit samāpattau hetur iti |

Yamaguchi's Japanese translation

その中、樂受はそれと和合して離れざらんとの渇愛の因なり。 苦〔受〕はそれと和合せず離れんとの渇愛の因なり。 非苦非樂〔受〕は或等至に於てそれの分位を愛樂する渇愛、及び、それと和合して離れざらんとの愛の因なり。 (references/mavt-ja.txt lines 3792–3794)

Translation

"[Pleasant vedanā is] the cause of [the craving] for non-separation from it. Painful [vedanā] is the cause of the craving for non-encounter [with it] and separation [from it]. Neither-painful-nor-pleasant [vedanā] is, in some samāpatti, the cause of the craving for staying in that state and of the craving for non-separation from it."

§33 — Sa duḥkha-vedanayā spṛṣṭaḥ kāma-sukham abhinandati iti vacanāt VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (sūtra citation + MAVbh paraphrase)

sa [Tib. 108, b] duḥkha-vedanayā spṛṣṭaḥ kāma-sukham abhinandati iti vacanāt duḥkhā api vedanā sukhāya tṛṣṇāyā hetur bhavati | tat-parīkṣayā samudaya-satyam avatarati | (p. 169; OCR lines 6910–6913, with Yamaguchi fn. 1)

Apparatus note (Yamaguchi fn. 1)

fn. 1: MS reads spaṣṭaḥ; Tib. reg-na (= spṛṣṭaḥ). Yamaguchi adopts the Tibetan reading spṛṣṭaḥ — clearly correct in context (the sūtra has spṛṣṭa, "touched [by]").

The sūtra citation

Sūtra-citation: vedanā → kāma-sukha-abhinandana The phrase sa duḥkha-vedanayā spṛṣṭaḥ kāma-sukham abhinandati is from the standard Sūtra-piṭaka discussion of the avidyā-saṃsparśa → vedanā → tṛṣṇā chain. Cited e.g. in Vasubandhu's Abhidharmakośabhāṣya ch. 9 (cf. Yamaguchi Japanese fn. 3837 referencing AKBh ch. 9 = T 30.284). The Pāli parallel is at Saṃyutta-Nikāya 36.6 (Salla-sutta).

Yamaguchi vs. proposed

Yamaguchi 1934 as printed
sa duḥkhavedanayā spṛṣṭaḥ kāmasukham abhinandatīti vacanāt duḥkhāpi vedanā sukhāya tṛṣṇāyā hetur bhavati | tatparīkṣayā samudayasatyam avatarati |
Proposed re-edited text
"sa duḥkha-vedanayā spṛṣṭaḥ kāma-sukham abhinandati" iti vacanāt, duḥkhā api vedanā sukhāya tṛṣṇāyā hetur bhavati | tat-parīkṣayā samudaya-satyam avatarati |

Yamaguchi's Japanese translation

「彼苦受に觸れたるとき欲樂を喜す」との語あるが故に、苦受も渇愛の因となる。彼を観ずることによりて集諦に入る。 (references/mavt-ja.txt lines 3795–3796)

Translation

"On the strength of the [sūtra-]saying: '[the unlearned ordinary person,] when touched by painful vedanā, delights in kāma-sukha [= sensual pleasure],' even painful vedanā too is a cause of craving — [namely] of craving for pleasure. By examining it, [the practitioner] enters the truth of origin."

§34 — Sa hi vedanāṃ svarūpataḥ kāraṇataḥ … upaparīkṣamāṇaḥ VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (MS + brief italics)

sa hi vedanāṃ svarūpataḥ kāraṇataḥ kāryato 'nityato duḥkhataś ca upaparīkṣamāṇaḥ samudaya-satyam avatarati | tṛṣṇāṃ tat-kāryatvena avagacchati iti arthaḥ | (p. 169; OCR lines 6913–6916, with Yamaguchi fn. 2)

Apparatus note (Yamaguchi fn. 2)

fn. 2: MS reads kāraṇato; Tib. ḥbras-bu … ñid-du (= kāryataḥ). Yamaguchi suggests the MS lost kāryataḥ and the Tib. lost kāraṇataḥ — both readings should stand together, parallel to a similar list at p. 170 line 11. Yamaguchi accordingly inserts both.

Structure

Five-fold examination of vedanā as the route into samudaya-satya:

  1. svarūpataḥ — by its own nature
  2. kāraṇataḥ — by its cause (= avidyā-saṃsparśa)
  3. kāryataḥ — by its effect (= tṛṣṇā)
  4. anityataḥ — by its impermanence
  5. duḥkhataḥ — by its painfulness

The gloss tṛṣṇāṃ tat-kāryatvena avagacchati ("[the practitioner] cognizes tṛṣṇā as [vedanā's] effect") explains specifically how the kāryataḥ-examination opens onto samudaya-satya.

Yamaguchi vs. proposed (no change apart from Yamaguchi's own fn. 2 emendation)

Yamaguchi 1934 as printed (with his fn. 2 added back)
sa hi vedanāṁ svarūpataḥ kāraṇataḥ [kāryato] 'nityato duḥkhataç copaparīkṣamāṇaḥ samudayasatyam avatarati | tṛṣṇāṁ tatkāryatvenāvagacchatīty arthaḥ |
Proposed re-edited text
sa hi vedanāṃ svarūpataḥ kāraṇataḥ kāryato 'nityato duḥkhataś ca upaparīkṣamāṇaḥ samudaya-satyam avatarati | "tṛṣṇāṃ tat-kāryatvena avagacchati" iti arthaḥ |

Yamaguchi's Japanese translation

彼〔修行者〕受を、自性と因と果とより、また無常及び苦なりと観察するとき集諦に入る。渇愛を彼〔受〕の果體として悟了すとの義なり。 (references/mavt-ja.txt lines 3797–3798)

Translation

"For he, examining vedanā by its self-nature, by its cause, by its effect, by its impermanence, and by its painfulness, enters the truth of origin. The sense is: '[he] cognizes tṛṣṇā as [vedanā's] effect.'"

§35 — Tṛṣṇā hi sūtre samudaya-satyam ity uktā (sūtra-citation) VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (italicised sūtra-reference)

tṛṣṇā hi sūtre samudaya-satyam ity uktā | ko duḥkha-samudayaḥ | paṭhyate "tṛṣṇā pāunarbhavikā ānandā rāga- sahagatā" iti vistaraḥ | evaṃ vedanā-smṛtyupasthānena samudaya-satyam avatarati | (p. 169; OCR lines 6916–6918)

The sūtra citation

Dharmacakra-pravartana-sūtra / second noble truth definition The classical definition: yā 'yaṃ tṛṣṇā pāunarbhavikī nandī-rāga-sahagatā tatra-tatra-abhinandinī ("the craving that is rebirth-producing, accompanied by delight-and-passion, delighting here and there"). Pāli at Saṃyutta-Nikāya 56.11 (Dhammacakkappavattana-sutta) and elsewhere (yāyaṃ taṇhā ponobhavikā nandīrāgasahagatā tatratatrābhinandinī, cf. Yamaguchi Japanese fn. 3850 quoting MN 141). Cited verbatim across Yogācāra / Abhidharma literature.

Apparatus note (Yamaguchi fn. 3)

fn. 3: MS reads °viveka; Tib. yaṅ-ḥbyuṅ-baḥi (= pāunarbhavika). Yamaguchi adopts the Tibetan reading — clearly correct, since the sūtra-text is standard.

Yamaguchi vs. proposed

Yamaguchi 1934 as printed
tṛṣṇā hi sūtre samudayasatyam ity uktā | ko duḥkhasamudayaḥ | paṭhyate tṛṣṇā pāunarbhavikānandā rāgasahagateti vistaraḥ | evaṁ vedanāsmṛtyupasthānena samudayasatyam avatarati |
Proposed re-edited text
"tṛṣṇā hi sūtre samudaya-satyam" ity uktā | "ko duḥkha-samudayaḥ? paṭhyate: tṛṣṇā pāunarbhavikā ānandā rāga-sahagatā" iti vistaraḥ | evaṃ vedanā-smṛtyupasthānena samudaya-satyam avatarati |

Yamaguchi's Japanese translation

實に渇愛は經中にも集諦なりと説かれたり。 云何が苦の集なる。 後有を喜ぶ渇愛にして貪と倶なるものなり等と誦せらる。 かくの如く受念處によりて集諦に入る。 (references/mavt-ja.txt lines 3800–3803)

Translation

"For in the sūtra, tṛṣṇā is said to be the truth of origin. [As the sūtra has it:] 'What is the origin of suffering? It is recited: the craving that is rebirth-producing, accompanied by delight and passion' — and so on. Thus, by the foundation-of-mindfulness on vedanā, [the practitioner] enters the truth of origin."

§36 — Ātmābhiniveśa-vastu cittam (citta-smṛtyupasthāna opens) VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (MAVbh pratīka + gloss)

ātmābhiniveśa-vastu cittam iti anyasya nityādi-guṇa-viśiṣṭasya ātmano 'bhāvāt ātmābhiniveśa-ālambanaṃ cittam eva | tasmāt tat-parīkṣayā nirodha-satyam avatarati śāntatvena | (p. 169; OCR lines 6920–6922)

The MAVbh source

Vasubandhu MAVbh ad IV.1 — local references/SA_T06_vmvkbh_u.txt line 812; indexed in SA_T06_vmvkbh_u. ātmābhiniveśavastu cittaṃ tatparīkṣayā nirodhasatyam avataraty ātmocchedabhayāpagamāt / Verbatim MAVbh. Sthiramati glosses each piece: "citta is the locus of ātma-clinging [pratīka] — because there is no other ātman endowed with the qualities of permanence etc., the ālambana of ātma-clinging is citta itself."

Yamaguchi vs. proposed (no change)

Yamaguchi 1934 as printed
ātmābhiniveçavastu cittam iti anyasya nityādiguṇaviçiṣṭasya ātmano 'bhāvād ātmābhiniveçālambanaṁ cittam eva | tasmāt tatparīkṣayā nirodhasatyam avatarati çāntatvena |
Proposed re-edited text
"ātmābhiniveśa-vastu cittam" iti — anyasya nityādi-guṇa-viśiṣṭasya ātmano 'bhāvād ātmābhiniveśa-ālambanaṃ cittam eva | tasmāt tat-parīkṣayā nirodha-satyam avatarati śāntatvena |

Yamaguchi's Japanese translation

我を執著する依處は心なりと云ふ、常等の殊勝なる德有る我は別に無きが故に我執の所縁は心のみ。 されば彼〔心〕を觀察することによりて滅諦を寂靜性として悟入す。 (references/mavt-ja.txt lines 3804–3805)

Translation

"[MAVbh:] 'citta is the locus of clinging-to-self' — because there is no other self-endowed-with-the-qualities-of- permanence-and-so-on, the object of self-clinging is citta itself. Therefore, by examining it, [the practitioner] enters the truth of cessation [understood] as quiescence."

§37 — Atra eva kāraṇam āha: ātmoccheda-bhaya-apagamād iti VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text (gloss on MAVbh continuation)

atra eva kāraṇam āha | ātmoccheda-bhaya-apagamād iti | citte hi anityatvādi-parīkṣayā ātmābhiniveśo vyāvartate duḥkha-saṃjñā pravartate | ataś ca ātmoccheda-bhaya-apagamād duḥkha-mātrasya ucchedān nirodha-satyaṃ śāntatvena avatarati iti | (p. 169; OCR lines 6922–6926, with Yamaguchi fn. 4)

Apparatus note (Yamaguchi fn. 4)

fn. 4: MS reads śāntatvaṃ cāvatarati; Tib. [ḥgog-paḥi bden-pa-la] çi-ba-ñid-du hjug-pa-ste (= "[enters] the [truth of cessation] as quiescence"). Yamaguchi restores the longer Tibetan-attested reading duḥkha-mātrasya ucchedāt nirodha-satyaṃ śāntatvena avatarati.

Structure

The why-question and answer for citta→nirodha:

  1. By examining citta for impermanence etc., the ātma-abhiniveśa ceases and the perception of suffering arises.
  2. Therefore, with the fear of self-annihilation gone (= MAVbh's ātmoccheda-bhaya-apagamāt), and through the cessation of mere-suffering (Yamaguchi's reconstruction duḥkha-mātrasya ucchedāt), [the practitioner] enters the truth of cessation as quiescence.

Yamaguchi vs. proposed (Yamaguchi's reconstruction stands)

Yamaguchi 1934 as printed
atrāiva kāraṇam āha | ātmocchedabhayāpagamād iti | citte hy anityatvādiparīkṣayā'tmābhiniveço vyāvartate duḥkhasaṁjñā pravartate | ataç cātmocchedabhayāpagamād duḥkhamātrāsyocchedān nirodhasatyaṁ çāntatvenāvataratīti |
Proposed re-edited text
atra eva kāraṇam āha — "ātmoccheda-bhaya-apagamād" iti | citte hi anityatvādi-parīkṣayā ātmābhiniveśo vyāvartate duḥkha-saṃjñā pravartate | ataś ca ātmoccheda-bhaya-apagamād duḥkha-mātrasya ucchedān nirodha-satyaṃ śāntatvena avatarati iti |

Yamaguchi's Japanese translation

此處に因を設けり、我の断絶することに對する怖を離るが故にと。 所以は心を無常等と觀察することによりて我執止息し苦想起るが故なり。 されば我の断絶することに對する怖を離るゝにより、 苦のみの斷絶なるが故に滅諦を寂靜として悟入すと。 (references/mavt-ja.txt lines 3806–3808)

Translation

"[The bhāṣyakāra] states the very reason for this [pratīka]: 'because of the removal of the fear of self-annihilation.' For, through the examination of citta as impermanent etc., the self-clinging ceases and the perception of [it as] suffering arises. Therefore, through the removal of the fear of self-annihilation, by the cessation of mere-suffering, [the practitioner] enters the truth of cessation [understood] as quiescence."

§39 — Dharma-parīkṣayā … mārga-satyam avatarati (dharma section opens) VERIFIED

Yamaguchi text

dharma-parīkṣayā sāṃkleśika-vyāvadānika- dharma-asammohān mārga-satyam avatarati iti | tatra sāṃkleśikā dharmāḥ kleśa-upakleśāḥ | [Tib. 109, a] vyāvadānikās tu kleśa-upakleśa-pratipakṣa- … (p. 169 → 170; OCR lines 6929–6931, continues on page 208)

The MAVbh source

Vasubandhu MAVbh ad IV.1 — local references/SA_T06_vmvkbh_u.txt line 813; indexed in SA_T06_vmvkbh_u. dharma-parīkṣayā sāṃkleśika-vaiyavadānika- dharma-asammohāt / mārga-satyam avataraty ataḥ / Note MAVbh has vaiyavadānika- (longer form, with vṛddhi); Sthiramati uses vyāvadānika- (shorter form, no vṛddhi). This is just an orthographic variant — both forms are attested in Yogācāra Sanskrit.

Structure

Opens the dharma-smṛtyupasthāna → mārga-satya section. The two classes of dharmas to be distinguished:

  • sāṃkleśika dharmas = kleśa-upakleśāḥ (defilements and secondary defilements).
  • vyāvadānika dharmas = kleśa-upakleśa-pratipakṣa- [their counteragents] — continues on p. 208.

Yamaguchi vs. proposed (no change)

Yamaguchi 1934 as printed
dharmaparīkṣayā sāṁkleçikavyāvadānikadharmāsammohān mārgasatyam avataratīti | tatra sāṁkleçikā dharmāḥ kleçopakleçāḥ | vyāvadānikās tu kleçopakleçapratipakṣa-…
Proposed re-edited text
"dharma-parīkṣayā sāṃkleśika-vyāvadānika-dharma-asammohān mārga-satyam avatarati" iti | tatra sāṃkleśikā dharmāḥ kleśa-upakleśāḥ | vyāvadānikās tu kleśa-upakleśa-pratipakṣa-… [continues p. 208]

Yamaguchi's Japanese translation

法を觀察することによりて雑染と清浄との法に對して愚迷せざるが故に道諦に入ると云ふ、その中、雑染法は煩惱と隨煩惱となり。 然るに清浄〔法〕は煩惱と隨煩惱との對治となれる… (references/mavt-ja.txt lines 3810–3811)

Translation

"'By the examination of dharmas, through non-confusion regarding defiling (sāṃkleśika) and purifying (vyāvadānika) dharmas, [the practitioner] enters the truth of the path.' Among these, the defiling dharmas are the kleśas and upakleśas. The purifying [dharmas] are the counteragents to those defilements and secondary defilements… [continues on p. 208]"

📝 noteIn the second chapter of the Madhyāntavibhāgaṭīkā (Sthiramati), Sthiramati discusses these bases of clining: Lack of skill with regard to the base is an obstacle with regard to the establishments of mindfulness.
