This site presents an ongoing re-edition of Sthiramati's Madhyāntavibhāgaṭīkā (MAVT), based on Susumu Yamaguchi's editio princeps (Nagoya, 1934). The goal is not to retype Yamaguchi but to interrogate his reconstructions: every italicised Sanskrit passage in his print is a conjecture filled into a manuscript-lacuna, and most can now be re-examined against the canonical Tibetan MAVT, the underlying Vasubandhu bhāṣya, the Maitreya kārikā, Sthiramati's other works, and cross-tract parallels via the DharmaMitra corpus.

For each Yamaguchi-page below, open the edition to read the sentence-by-sentence apparatus and proposed emendations. The original scan is embedded at the top of every edition file. You can also open any raw page image on its own.

Methodology & status

The full editing methodology — search protocol, what counts as admissible evidence, status tracker per page, lessons learned — is in mavt-reedition-workflow.md.

About this project. The editorial work is produced by an Anthropic Claude agent driving the DharmaMitra /primary/ search and /cat-translate/ APIs over many sessions. The sentence-by-sentence apparatus is machine-generated and has not yet been reviewed by a human philologist; the original Yamaguchi text and the Tibetan witnesses are reproduced from public-domain / canonical sources. If you propose readings of your own or spot errors, please open an issue or pull request on GitHub.
