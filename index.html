This site presents an ongoing re-edition of chapter 4 of Sthiramati's Madhyāntavibhāgaṭīkā — the chapter on the cultivation of antidotes (pratipakṣa-bhāvanā: the 37 bodhipakṣya-dharmāḥ, beginning with the four smṛtyupasthāna-s) — based on Susumu Yamaguchi's editio princeps (Nagoya, 1934) and on Sebastian Nehrdich's TEI-style critical edition.

Every sentence is presented in three rows (Sanskrit / Tibetan / English) with the full philological apparatus inline: variants are collected by witness sigla (Ms, Na, Y, Pa, St for Sanskrit; D, P for Tibetan), inline Sanskrit and Tibetan quotations are clickable links to a search on DharmaMitra / DharmaNexus, and editorial notes & parallel-passage citations are tucked behind 📋 / 📝 expanders so the main text stays readable.

For each Yamaguchi-page below, open the edition to read the sentence-by-sentence apparatus and proposed emendations. The original scan is embedded at the top of every edition file. You can also open any raw page image on its own, or browse all 321 PDF pages from the link further down.

Methodology & status

The editing methodology — search protocol, what counts as admissible evidence, lessons learned — is documented in mavt-reedition-workflow.md. The protocol is general and applies equally to chapter 4 and to other chapters that may follow.

About this project. The editorial work is produced by an Anthropic Claude agent driving the DharmaMitra /primary/ search and /cat-translate/ APIs over many sessions. The sentence-by-sentence apparatus is machine-generated and has not yet been reviewed by a human philologist; the original Yamaguchi text and the Tibetan witnesses are reproduced from public-domain / canonical sources. If you propose readings of your own or spot errors, please open an issue or pull request on GitHub.
